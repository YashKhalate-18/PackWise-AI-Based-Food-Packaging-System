Most packaging is chosen by habit, not by the food.

A packet that suits dry rice can ruin fresh produce. Small producers rarely have a packaging expert to ask.

Spoilage

Wrong barrier or breathability shortens shelf life and raises post-harvest loss.

Waste

Over-packaging and non-recyclable plastics add cost and harm the environment.

No guidance

Farmers and small food businesses have little access to technical advice.

From commodity to packaging choice in three steps

  1. Describe your product

    Pick the commodity and enter moisture, storage temperature, transport time, and budget.

  2. The model compares materials

    PackWise weighs barrier properties, breathability, cost, and environmental impact for each option.

  3. Get a clear recommendation

    See the best material, a shelf-life estimate, and a greener alternative when one performs as well.

What PackWise gives you

Material recommendation

Suggestions matched to each commodity, from HDPE and PET to paper and biodegradable films.

Shelf-life estimate

See how long your product should last in the packaging you choose.

Cost and eco comparison

Compare options side by side and pick the trade-off that fits your business.

Plain-language reasons

Every result explains why a material was chosen, so you can trust it.
